Acne & Scar Management
01. Acne & Scar Care

Acne & Post-Acne Scar Management

Addressing active breakouts early helps reduce long-term scarring while calming redness and sensitivity.

What It Involves

Skin barrier evaluation, targeted medical topicals or oral therapy when indicated, comedone extraction, salicylic or mandelic peels, and scar resurfacing planning.

Potential Benefits

  • Helps reduce active inflammatory breakouts
  • Gradually softens shallow post-acne texture
  • Minimises risk of new post-acne dark marks

Consultation Note: Dr. Verma reviews current skincare products, hormonal triggers, and past medications before selecting a gentle step-by-step regimen.

Laser Skin Treatments
02. Laser Dermatology

Precision Laser Skin & Hair Procedures

Medical laser sessions calibrated to melanin levels across diverse skin tones for predictable, comfort-conscious care.

What It Involves

Pre-procedure patch assessment, protective eyewear, cooled laser handpiece passes for hair reduction, toning, or resurfacing, followed by soothing post-care.

Potential Benefits

  • Progressive reduction of unwanted facial or body hair
  • Targeted support for textural irregularities
  • Short outpatient sessions with minimal recovery time

Consultation Note: Session counts and energy settings depend on hair thickness, skin tone, and recent sun exposure assessed during consultation.

Pigmentation & Melasma Care
03. Tone & Clarity

Pigmentation, Melasma & Sun-Spot Care

Safe, gradual tone correction that prioritises barrier stability rather than harsh bleaching agents.

What It Involves

Dermatoscopic pigment depth mapping, medical depigmenting formulations, mild brightening peels, and structured broad-spectrum sun protection.

Potential Benefits

  • Supports a brighter, more even complexion
  • Avoids steroid-induced rebound pigmentation
  • Tailored year-round UV protection for UK weather

Consultation Note: Pigmentation often requires steady home care alongside spaced clinical visits for sustainable improvement.

Anti-Ageing & Skin Rejuvenation
04. Rejuvenation & Anti-Ageing

Anti-Ageing & Skin Rejuvenation

Restorative clinical care designed to support skin hydration, smoothness, and natural vitality.

What It Involves

Clinical hydration facials, microneedling collagen support, superficial resurfacing peels, and physician-guided retinoid or antioxidant routines.

Potential Benefits

  • Refreshes dull, dehydrated skin texture
  • Softens early fine lines around eyes and mouth
  • Preserves natural facial movement and balance

Consultation Note: Programmes are customised around upcoming events, comfort tolerance, and realistic long-term skin ageing goals.

Hair & Scalp Dermatology
05. Trichology

Hair Fall & Scalp Health Programmes

Diagnostic scalp care identifying shedding patterns, follicle miniaturisation, and scalp inflammation.

What It Involves

Magnified trichoscopy scan, blood-work guidance when needed, medical hair-growth topicals, dandruff control, and supportive growth-factor scalp sessions.

Potential Benefits

  • Clarifies the underlying cause of sudden or chronic shedding
  • Calms flaking, itching, and scalp buildup
  • Supports healthier hair shaft strength over time

Consultation Note: Hair cycles respond gradually over several months; follow-up trichoscopy helps track density objectively.

Medical Dermatology
06. Clinical Dermatology

Medical Dermatology & Skin Checks

Comprehensive diagnosis and ongoing care for allergic, inflammatory, infectious, and nail conditions.

What It Involves

Dedicated MD consultation covering eczema, psoriasis, urticaria, fungal infections, vitiligo, paediatric rashes, and suspicious mole evaluation.

Potential Benefits

  • Accurate diagnosis instead of trial-and-error OTC creams
  • Safe symptom relief for sensitive and paediatric skin
  • Clear guidance on flare triggers and daily barrier care

Consultation Note: Please bring photos of past flare-ups or lists of current creams to help Dr. Verma review your history thoroughly.
